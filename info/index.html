Information

A full breakdown of every feature and change in Season 3 of L&M.
This page is updated as new features are added, so check back often!

Major Changes

⚔️

Class System

Players can now pick a specialisation from Bilbo in-game. Each class unlocks unique perks and mechanics — Enchanter, Miner, Lumberjack, Blacksmith, and more coming.

Explore classes →
💎

Diamond Upgrade System

Upgrading gear is now harder! Diamond upgrades have been added to increase the difficulty of reaching the max tier — this keeps progression meaningful for longer.

💀

Wither Fight Rework

The Wither has been rebalanced to be more challenging.

  • — Max health increased to 400 HP
  • — Maximum damage per hit is 20 HP
  • — If the Wither doesn't move for 4 seconds:
    • · Creates a large explosion — blast radius 5
    • · Teleports a few blocks away
    • · Designed to break Wither cages
  • — Withers named "static":
    • · Do not use the anti-cage mechanic
    • · Do not drop a Nether Star
  • — When damaged, may summon reinforcements:
    • · Wither Skeletons (Stone Sword, no drops)
    • · Wither Bats
  • — Reinforcements are capped at:
    • · 12 Wither Skeletons
    • · 12 Wither Bats
  • — Reinforcements automatically die when the Wither dies
  • — Creates an explosion when reaching 50% health — blast radius 5
  • — Movement speed increased by 0.15
  • — Resistance increased by 0.05
💫

Exp Drops

EXP from mobs is now context-aware — where and how a mob was killed affects how much XP it drops.

  • — EXP adjusts based on mob context:
    • · If from a spawner:
      • › Default → 50% EXP (rounded randomly, never 0) + chance to drop EXP bottles
      • › If 12+ living mobs within 2 blocks → 0 EXP (anti-massive-farm)
      • › If alive less than 90 seconds → 50% EXP (rounded randomly, never 0)
      • › Otherwise (lived long, not farmed) → 200% EXP + chance to drop EXP bottles
  • — EXP bottles may drop when allowed:
    • · Roughly 1 bottle per 7 EXP
    • · 80% chance to drop any bottles at all
🔮

Enchantment Overhaul

We have several custom enchants, ranging from Vein Miner to XP Amplifier. The Enchanter class can roll enchantments using Mythical Dust.

See all enchantments →
🌟

Mythical Dust

A new resource dropped by ores when you are a Miner. Used at the Enchanting Table to roll custom enchantments exclusively via the Enchanter class.

🐉

Ender Dragon Fight Rework

The Ender Dragon has been rebalanced to be more challenging.

  • — Max HP is 600
  • — Max damage is 20 HP in one hit
  • — Attacks faster and more targeted at you
  • — Summons angry endermen and phantoms randomly
  • — Resistant to explosions
  • — While the dragon is alive:
    • · Phantoms spawn above any player further than 100 blocks away on any x/z axis — they just keep spawning, with no drops
  • — Dragon Fireballs spawn 1–3 silverfish on impact
  • — End crystals auto-respawn after 4–8 minutes — one at a time, cooldown resets after each
⛏️

Faster Mining

Two players mining the same block will boost the breaking speed!

Major Features

🏰

Kingdoms

Claim and name your own piece of the map. Kingdoms are visible on the live map and show ownership — build your empire or find your neighbours.

View the live map! →
🍳

Custom Foods & Farmers Delight

Dozens of new food items alongside Farmers Delight integration. Cooking and eating is now a genuine part of the experience.

🍪

Scale Cookies

Eat a Scale Cookie to change your player height — or feed one to any entity to resize them too!

  • — Eating one gives you a random scale
  • — Can also be fed to any entity:
    • · Shift + Right-click the entity while holding the cookie
    • · Gives them a random scale, just like eating one yourself
    • · You can override their scale repeatedly — unlike your own
🖼️

Wallpapers

Stick thin decorative panels onto any wall, floor, or ceiling block — purely visual, fully removable, and reusable.

  • — Placing
    • · Hold a wallpaper item and right-click any solid wall, floor, or ceiling block
    • · Sticks flush to that face — a thin panel covering the whole block like tiling
    • · Doesn't replace the block itself — the wall stays solid underneath
  • — Removing
    • · Left-click with Shears, or Shift + Left-click while holding the same wallpaper item
    • · Pops off as a dropped item — pick it up and reuse it elsewhere
  • — Chameleon Wallpaper — special variant
    • · Has no fixed pattern — copies the texture of any block you feed it
    • · Shift + Right-click it with a block twice in quick succession to apply
    • · Becomes "wood plank wallpaper", "stone brick wallpaper", etc.
    • · One item can cover any texture — no need for a separate type per block
  • — Baseboards
    • · Thin trim strips that pair with wallpaper — like skirting boards along the bottom of a wall
    • · Shift while placing to lay them flat on the floor as a trim strip instead
🧩

Spawner Fragments

Break spawners with Silk Touch to collect fragments and craft your own placeable spawners.

  • — Works hand-in-hand with the Cagebreaker enchantment
  • — Breaking a spawner with Silk Touch yields fragments:
    • · Each break gives 1–2 fragments
  • — Combine x8 Fragments + x1 Empty Soul → craft x1 placeable Spawner
🌌

Exploration Dimension

A dedicated dimension apart from our main world for anyone to explore and loot. Simply boat out to the world border and into the new dimension!

🪙

Coin Economy

We have a coin-based currency system. Trade, earn, and spend coins across the server economy.

  • — Coin Values
    • · 🥇 Gold Coin = 100 Copper Coins (CC)
    • · 🥈 Silver Coin = 5 Copper Coins (CC)
    • · 🪙 Copper Coin = Base currency
    • · 🟤 Rusted Copper Coin = ¼ Copper Coin
    • · 🧩 Rusted Copper Coin Shard = ¹⁄₅₀ Rusted Copper Coin
  • — Converting Coins
    • · Shift + Right-click a Gold Coin → 20 Silver Coins
    • · Shift + Right-click a Silver Coin → 5 Copper Coins
    • · 20 Silver Coins → 1 Gold Coin
    • · 4 Rusted Copper Coins → 1 Copper Coin
    • · 50 Rusted Copper Coin Shards → 1 Rusted Copper Coin
  • — Finding Rusted Coin Shards
    • · 🎣 Obtainable while fishing
    • · Very rare — roughly a 1 in 5,000 chance
🔐

Locks

You can craft a Lock & Key in-game to lock mostly anything! Protect your chests, doors, and even crafting tables from pesky players.

  • — Locking a Block
    • · Hold a Lock in your main hand and the Key in your off-hand
    • · Shift + Right-click the block to lock it
    • · The lock is consumed and permanently bound to that key
  • — Sharing Access
    • · Give another player the Key
    • · They right-click with it once to gain access to every lock bound to that key
    • · Get the key back when you're done!
  • — Revoking Access
    • · Hold the Key and Shift + Right-click
    • · This removes all shared players from that key
  • — Tips
    • · You can have multiple keys for different groups of locks
    • · Breaking a locked block drops the lock item
    • · Anyone can break a locked block, but doing so without permission is griefing — report it to staff
📦

Lootr

Every player gets their own independent loot from structure chests — no more racing to loot dungeons first.

Trade Changes

📚

Librarian Trades

Enchanted book trades have been reworked with new costs and requirements.

  • — All enchanted books are now sold at Level I
  • — Trades now require Lapis Lazuli + a Book
  • — The amount of Lapis depends on the enchantment's rarity
  • — Maximum cost is 64 Lapis
✝️

Cleric Changes

Cleric trades updated with better value exchanges.

  • — 1 Emerald → 1 Bottle o' Enchanting
  • — 1 Emerald → 2 Charcoal (replaces the Redstone trade)
⚒️

Equipment Trades

Enchanted and enchantable equipment trades have been reworked.

  • — 20% Chance — Cursed Bargain
    • · Costs the item's crafting material + 4–6 Emeralds
    • · Comes with Curse of Unchanging Fate I
  • — 80% Chance — Normal Trade
    • · Diamond gear requires a Diamond Upgrade Smithing Template
    • · Iron, Gold, and Diamond gear require their respective crafting material
    • · Enchanted gear also requires 1–3 Lapis Lazuli
    • · Unenchanted gear instead costs 4–6 Emeralds
🦙

Wandering Trader

Now always offers one special upgrade trade, randomly choosing between:

  • — 📖 Stabilized Enchanted Book
    • · Upgrades an Electrified Enchanted Book into a Stabilized Enchanted Book
  • — ⛏️ Stabilized Pickaxe
    • · Upgrades an Electrified Golden Pickaxe into a Stabilized Pickaxe
