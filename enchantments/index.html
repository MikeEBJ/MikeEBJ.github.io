🔮 Custom Enchantments

Discover the unique enchantments available on the L&M server.
Each enchantment adds new gameplay dynamics, challenges, or fun mechanics.

🔧 Indestructibuddy

Charged

Makes an item unbreakable!
Very difficult to gain.
Can be only gained from wandering traders!

Max Level: I

⛏️ Cagebreaker

Charged

When used on a spawner it causes it to drop the spawn-egg inside!
Very difficult to gain.
Can be only gained from wandering traders!

Max Level: I

🧲 Items Like Me

Utility

Whenever you kill/mine with this enchantment on your item, it will instantly teleport the item into your inventory.
If your inventory is full and there is nothing else it can stack into it will just drop as normal.

Max Level: I

🪓 One Chop Wonder

Utility

Break a single log to bring down the entire tree!
The amount broken increases by enchant level.
It will prefer Side → Diagonal → Vertical Diagonal → Vertical.
It will never expand downwards

Max Level: VIII

🌀 Shredder

Utility

Breaks blocks into their "next stage" automatically (stone → cobble, logs → planks, etc).

Additional crushable conversions:

  • Andesite, Diorite, Granite → Gravel
  • Sand, Red Sand, Gravel → Clay / Sand
  • Stone Bricks, Nether/Deepslate/Blackstone Bricks → Cracked variants
  • Stone Slab → Cobblestone Slab
  • Stone Stairs → Cobblestone Stairs
  • Soul Soil → Soul Sand

Max Level: X

✨ XP Amplifier

Utility

Gain more XP from mobs, blocks, and produce more bottles o' enchanting.

Max Level: V

🧠 Miner's Instinct

Utility

Breaks connected ores while mining. Each level breaks more!

Max Level: V

🛡️ Durashield™

Utility

If your tool breaks while having this, the level of the enchantment will reduce by 1.

Max Level: X

🙃 Curse of Butterfingers

Curse

Gives a chance for the tool to just slip out of your hand!

Max Level: X

💥 Curse of Shattering

Curse

Your tool will instantly break upon any use — Durashield™ protects against this!

Max Level: I

🧠 Curse of the Slow Learner

Curse

Prevents EXP gain from entities.

Max Level: I

🛠️ Curse of Rusted Tools

Curse

Prevents repairing the item in anvils or with mending.

Max Level: I

🌀 Curse of Unchanging Fate

Curse

Prevents an item from being upgraded or reforged at anvils or smithing tables.

Max Level: I
