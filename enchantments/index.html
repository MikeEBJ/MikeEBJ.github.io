🔮 Custom Enchantments

Unique enchantments that add new challenges and mechanics to the L&M server.

✨

Class Enchants

Six enchantments are only obtainable through the Enchanter class — place Mythical Dust in the lapis slot of an enchanting table to roll them. Some also require a specific class equipped to use them (shown by a class badge on the card).

⚡ Charged

Rare and powerful. Obtainable only from wandering traders.

🔧 Indestructibuddy

Charged

Makes an item completely unbreakable. Very difficult to obtain.

Max level: I Wandering trader only.

⛏️ Cagebreaker

Charged

When used on a spawner, causes it to drop the spawn egg inside.

Max level: I Wandering trader only.

🔧 Utility

Quality-of-life enchantments available through standard enchanting.

🧲 Items Like Me

Utility

Kills and mining instantly teleport drops into your inventory. Drops normally if your inventory is full.

Max level: I

🪓 One Chop Wonder

Utility

Break a single log to bring down the entire tree. Amount broken increases per level. Prefers Side → Diagonal → Vertical — never expands downward.

Max level: VIII

🌀 Shredder

Utility

Breaks blocks into their next stage: stone → cobblestone, logs → planks, etc. Also converts stone bricks → cracked variants, sand/gravel → clay, soul soil → soul sand, and more.

Max level: X

✨ XP Amplifier

Utility

Gain more XP from mobs and blocks, and produce more bottles o' enchanting.

Max level: V

🧠 Miner's Instinct

Utility

Breaks connected ores while mining. Each level increases the vein size broken.

Max level: V

🛡️ Durashield™

Utility

When your tool would break, the enchantment level drops by 1 instead. Works against Curse of Shattering.

Max level: X

☠️ Curses

Negative effects — avoid if you can.

🙃 Curse of Butterfingers

Curse

Gives a random chance for the tool to slip out of your hand.

Max level: X

💥 Curse of Shattering

Curse

Your tool instantly breaks on first use. Durashield™ can protect against this.

Max level: I

🐢 Curse of the Slow Learner

Curse

Prevents XP gain from entities.

Max level: I

🛠️ Curse of Rusted Tools

Curse

Prevents repairing the item in an anvil or with Mending.

Max level: I

🌀 Curse of Unchanging Fate

Curse

Prevents the item from being upgraded or reforged at anvils or smithing tables.

Max level: I
