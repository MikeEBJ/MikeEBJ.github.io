Minecraft Server Rules

Please follow these rules to ensure a fair and enjoyable experience for everyone. Use common sense, be respectful, and keep the world beautiful.

1. No attacking others

You Heard Right. No Attacking Others Of Any Sort Unless You Get Their Stated Permissions.

2. Owning land

To Own Land You Must Have A Structure With A Purpose. Signs And Fences Alone Do Not Count.

3. Don't grief

No Breaking Anything That You Do Not Own. No Interacting With Levers/Buttons/Doors/Or Trapdoors Without Permission.

4. Don’t grief the overworld

Don't spam nerd poles or leave creeper holes. Clean up after yourself. Applies loosely in the Nether.

5. Don’t destroy trial chambers

Don’t break vaults or remove spawners in trial chambers.

6. Respect others

If Someone Asks You To Leave Them Alone, Leave Them Alone. Do Not Harass Others.

7. Public Farms

Farms are considered public unless clearly marked as private. Enclosures such as buildings, walls, or fences imply private ownership by default.

8. Banned players’ belongings

Items go to staff. Shared bases get handled via ticket.

9. Replant trees

Don't let others struggle to find wood, replant your trees!

10. Build pretty

Don't make abominations of a house, try to keep houses neat.

11. Theft is illegal

Stealing isn't cool, so don't do it.

12. Market Square

We have a Market Square for shops, use it!

13. No exploits

No dupes, hacks, or anything of the sort.

14. Manually play

Auto-clicking and stuff that assists gameplay is fine. But outright botting the game isn't fair

This basically means removing the human aspect isn't fair.

15. Lag machines

Don't make lag machines. Be mindful of what impact you create.

16. Chunk Loaders

Chunkloaders are banned. You can afk in chunks but you cannot create a loader.

Sever Bullying Policy

We're sick of bullies here. The more complaints we receive, the more severe the punishment.
Please, be respectful. Be kind. Let's keep the server a positive place for everyone.

Punishment Tiers

Tier 1 Verbal Warning
Tier 2 Server Timeout
Tier 3 Permanent Server Mute

(both in-game and on Discord)

Tier 4 Ban

These rules are subject to change. By playing on the server, you agree to follow them. Contact staff if you need clarification.
