⚖️ Minecraft Server Rules

Follow these rules for a smooth and enjoyable experience. Let's keep the server fun, fair, and respectful for everyone!

While these tags have no relation to any punishment scale,
they do signify the type of punishment given out for the given rule break.

  • L-1 - Low
  • L-2 - Med
  • L-3 - High

These tags signify the severity of the punishment if the rule is broken.

1. No Attacking Others

L-2

No attacking others of any sort unless you get their stated permissions. Everytime.

- And yes, we mean any sort, traps included.

2. Owning Land

L-1

To fully own land, you must have a structure with an actual purpose.

- Signs & Fences Only Count For 1 Week.

3. Don't Grief

L-3

- No breaking anything that you do not own.
- No interacting with levers/buttons/doors/trapdoors without permission
- Don't spam nerd poles or leave creeper holes.
Clean up after yourself.

4. Manually Play

L-2

Auto-clicking and stuff that assists gameplay is fine.
But outright botting the game isn't fair.
This basically means removing the human aspect isn't fair.

- Things like a mod that fishes for you is not fine

5. Respect Others

L-2

If someone asks you to leave them alone, leave them alone.
Do not harass others.

6. Public Farms

L-2

Crop Farms are considered public unless clearly marked as private.
Enclosures such as buildings, walls, or fences imply private ownership by default.

- All other farms are considered private by default.

7. Banned Players' Belongings

L-1

Items/homes will be auctioned off.
Shared bases get handled via ticket.

- All profits go to the goverment

8. Replant Trees

L-2

Don't let others struggle to find wood, replant your trees!

- Doesn't need to be in the exact spot

9. Build Pretty

L-1

Don't make abominations of a house, try to keep houses neat.

- If the build is found unpleasant, we will ask you to remove it.
- If not removed within a week, actions will be taken.

10. Theft is Illegal

L-3

Stealing isn't cool, so don't do it.

- No matter how small.

11. Market Island

L-2

Shops can be anywhere!

- List of rules in the Sand Castle.
- Actions will be taken within 3 days.

12. Lag Machines

L-3

Don't make lag machines.
Be mindful of the impact you create.

- If found or reported, the machine will be removed and you will be unwhitelisted.

13. Trial Chambers

L-2

Do not break vaults or remove spawners in the trial chambers.

14. No Exploiting

L-3

No dupes, hacks, or anything of the sort.

15. Chunk Loaders

L-2

Chunkloaders are banned. You can afk in chunks but you cannot create a loader.

- If found or reported, the chunk loader will be removed and you will be unwhitelisted.

16. Player Contracts

L-2

Contracts or agreements made by players are not and will not be enforced by staff.

- If issues arise, players must handle it themselves unless some other rule has been clearly broken

17. Elytra Limit

L-2

To encourage more players to explore we max the elytras you can own to 2!
This prevents the case where people have to explore thousands of blocks to even gain 1 elytra half.




These rules are subject to change. By playing on the server, you agree to follow them. Contact staff if you need clarification.

Sever Bullying Policy

We're sick of bullies here. The more complaints we receive, the more severe the punishment.
Please, be respectful. Be kind. Let's keep the server a positive place for everyone.

Punishment Tiers

Tier 1 Verbal Warning
Tier 2 Server Timeout
Tier 3 Permanent Server Mute

(both in-game and on Discord)

Tier 4 Ban
