Welcome to L&M

Just joined? This starter guide will walk you through installation, in-game setup, and how currency works on the server.

1. Installing the Modpack

Modrinth (Recommended)

The easiest and most reliable way to get started.

  1. Open the Modrinth Launcher
  2. Add a new modpack
  3. Search for L&M Essentials
  4. Install the latest version

CurseForge & Other Launchers

  1. Download the .zip from the latest version page
  2. Create a Fabric instance
  3. Drop all mods into the mods folder
  4. Launch the game

2. In-Game Tips

Voice Chat (Optional)

Press V in-game to open the Plasmo Voice configuration and set up your microphone. If you installed via Modrinth, your keybinds should already be sorted. Other launchers may require manual adjustment.

Spawn Shelter

Near spawn you’ll find a small hobo town. You’re welcome to take shelter in one of the falling-apart cardboard tents if needed. This is optional, but available to everyone.

Mining Dimension

Using a waystone, select L&M Shopping Island.

From there, you can access the Mining Dimension entrance located behind Jim & Jan’s cart. Follow the posted signs from the waystone to reach the entrance.

3. Currency System

Coins & Usage

Silver Coins are the main server currency. You can spend them at L&M Shopping Island.

On the island you’ll find Jim & Jan, the server bankers, operating out of a cart.

Earning Coins

  • Playtime: 1 Copper Coin per 30 minutes, max of 5/day (collect daily from Jim)
  • Voting: 1 Silver Coin per day for voting on PMC (collect daily from Jan)

Coin Conversion

You can convert coins directly in the air:

  • Right-click: Convert to a higher tier
  • Shift + Right-click: Convert to a lower tier

Rates: 5 Copper → 1 Silver, 20 Silver → 1 Gold
